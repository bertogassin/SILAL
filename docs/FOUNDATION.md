# Foundation

This frame is load-bearing. A future bank or insurer **plugs in**. They do not replace SILAL supply, the ledger, kinship, consent, or these flags.

| Invariant | Value |

| Referral | one level, capped, from the community fund |

| Insurance pay | never automatic |

| Yield | not a product |

## Bank room

Family custody pots sit on derived addresses `bank:pot:{id}`. Tokens move. They are not created. Withdrawal later needs family multisig; today the house that opened the pot can recall it locally.

A qard is a recorded transfer with no rate field. Repayment is a reverse transfer.

## Insurance room

Claims are hashed drafts against the genesis insurance fund (4%). A future mutual (ta'awun) adds council signatures. It does not mint, and it does not auto-pay.

## What this is not

Not a licensed bank. Not a licensed insurer. Not a yield vault. Not a social credit score. Not a replacement for the law of the land.
